Keys and ShortcutsKeyboard shortcuts and mouse actions in Planora
Planora · Document 3
October 2026

Every command in Planora is in the menus, but these keys make daily work faster. The single most useful one is Ctrl + K: it opens a search box where you can type the name of any command, view or task.

Mouse actions in the Gantt chart

ActionResult
Drag the middle of a bar left or rightMoves the task (an auto-scheduled task gets a “Start No Earlier Than” date)
Drag the right end of a barChanges the duration
Drag the left end of a barSets the % complete
Drag a bar up or down onto another taskLinks the two tasks (finish-to-start)
Double-click a bar or a row numberOpens Task Information
Hover over a barShows duration, start, finish, progress and slack
Ctrl + mouse wheelZoom in and out around the mouse
Shift + mouse wheelScroll the chart sideways
Click the arrow next to a phaseCollapse or expand the phase
Right-click a column headingAutoFilter: show only rows with chosen values

Typing shortcuts in the task table

You typePlanora understands
5d, 2w, 4h, 1mo5 days, 2 weeks, 4 hours, 1 month of working time
3ed3 elapsed (calendar) days, including weekends
4d?An estimated duration
0dA milestone
3 or 3FS+2d or 5SS-1dPredecessors: row number, link type and lag or lead
Crew[200%], Cement[40]Resources with units or quantities
Tip. Every change, typed or dragged, can be undone with Ctrl + Z.
